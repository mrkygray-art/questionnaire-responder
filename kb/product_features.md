# Product Features

## PRD-1.3 Visitor management
Visitor pre-registration with time-limited access is offered.

## PRD-3.1 Alarm integration
Intrusion and alarm panel integration is not supported.

## PRD-5.1 Support hours
Standard support hours are Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM Pacific Time. After-hours support by phone is available for Priority 1 issues only.

## PRD-5.2 Priority 1 definition
Priority 1 issues include, for example, a site that cannot secure its doors.
