# Approved Past Answers

## APA-01 Encryption in transit
Yes. All customer-facing and API traffic is encrypted with TLS 1.2 or higher.

## APA-02 Encryption at rest
Yes. Data at rest is encrypted with AES-256, and keys are managed in the infrastructure provider's key management service.

## APA-03 Single sign-on
Yes. The admin console supports SAML 2.0 and OpenID Connect single sign-on.

## APA-04 Multi-factor authentication
MFA is required for staff, and customer administrators can enforce MFA through their identity provider or the built-in console MFA.

## APA-06 Data residency
Customer data is hosted in United States regions only.

## APA-08 Backups
Database backups are taken daily and kept for 35 days.

## APA-12 Penetration testing
We commission an annual independent penetration test. A summary letter is available under NDA on request; detailed findings are not shared.
